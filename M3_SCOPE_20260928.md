# M3 re-analysis of stored predictions: scope fixed before computation

> Release copy. Internal process references (review sessions, internal names, local paths) were removed for publication; the analysis plan itself is unchanged. The SHA-256 of the file as committed and hashed by the original run is listed in `docs/PROVENANCE.md`.

## Origin, status and history

Planned on 2026-09-28 as item M3 (including its per-class item) of an internal evaluation of the manuscript, which recommended a re-analysis of the existing predictions before any new experiment.

This is a retrospective, descriptive analysis of ACCIDENT test predictions that have already been audited and re-analysed. It is not a confirmatory test.

- No model is trained and no inference is run.
- Frozen protocols, manifests, predictions and historical outputs of the audit are read-only.
- Outputs go to `outputs/m3_20260928/` (branch `research/reanalysis-m3-20260928`).
- All bootstrap intervals are conditional on the fixed classifier library. They do not reflect training randomness.

Drafting history: this version incorporates a pre-computation review. No M3 quantity was computed before this document was committed.

## Common settings

- **Systems**: the 168 nontrivial systems of the frozen library (primary pool, `outputs/r2b`), in three sets:
  - B: 27 base classifiers (families `clip` and `prefix`);
  - N: 63 noncommitment systems (B plus 36 post-processing arms);
  - A: all 168.
- **Trivial references**: the frozen trivial systems (majority class and uniform random), used only in D.
- **Data**:
  - test split: 1514 clips, unchanged manifest, source-video clusters as recorded;
  - development split: 102 clips, used only in C3;
  - training class proportions are read from the manifest.
- **Grid, horizons and weights**: as in `run002`.
  - Reading grid 0.25 s.
  - H = 10 s primary; 4 s and 21.5 s secondary.
  - Fixed cohorts E_H = {L+ ≥ H}.
  - Class-macro weights primary; clip weights in parallel.
  - Every quantity uses the same weights v as the RMSCD it is compared with.
- **Answers**: an explicit no-answer is wrong. Missing cached cells inside the video support stop the run.
- **Bootstrap**:
  - One shared source-cluster bootstrap over the test split: 2000 replicates, seed 20260928, the same draws for every system and cohort.
  - A replicate in which any analysed cohort lacks a class is redrawn; the number of redraws is reported.
  - Intervals are 2.5–97.5 percentiles, descriptive only. No test is added and no multiplicity control is claimed.
- **Ties and undefined values**:
  - Absolute differences below 1e-9 are ties. Ranks are broken by sorted system identifier.
  - A quantity undefined for a system in a replicate drops that system from that replicate only.
  - The number of such replicates is reported. An interval is reported only if at least 1900 of 2000 replicates define the statistic.
- **Pairs**: comparisons within a set use all unordered pairs (primary), with same-seed pairs as a secondary view. Pairs are enumerated directly, not through `run002`'s same-seed helper.

## A. Window-end-tied pairs without commitment rules (frozen audit)

**Inputs** (read-only):
- `outputs/r2b/gates/g4.json`: frozen tied pairs, Holm decisions and seed groups. This is the source used for the paper's Table 2.
- The per-system π0 records `outputs/r2b/metrics/<pi_hash>/<system>.json`: clip-weighted S_H curve, grid step, effective horizon.

**Terms and orientation**:
- Endpoint term of a system: H_eff (1 − A), with A = S_H(H_eff), the clip-weighted window-end accuracy.
- Delay term: the trapezoid area of S_H(H_eff) − S_H(δ) (Eq. 5).
- For a pair, γ = RMSCD(b) − RMSCD(a). Its endpoint component is H_eff (A_a − A_b) and its delay component is γ minus the endpoint component.
- Reorienting a pair reverses both components together.
- "Same sign": the delay component has the sign of γ.
- "Dominant": |delay| > |endpoint|.
- "Endpoint opposite": the endpoint component has the opposite sign to γ.
- Pairs with |γ| < 1e-12 are excluded and counted.

**Reproduction** (before new output), at all three horizons:
- Table 2 columns:
  - tied pairs;
  - heterogeneous pairs, cross-base, replicated groups;
  - delay-dominant;
  - both "without commitment rule" columns.
- H = 10 s text figures:
  - 2899 same-sign, 719 endpoint-opposite, medians 1.03 / 0.79 / 0.29 s;
  - 408 noncommitment heterogeneous pairs, 330 cross-base, 395 same-sign, 223 dominant, median 0.80 s;
  - 19 groups, of which 14 involve the first-round prefix-mean classifier.
- Integers must match exactly. Printed decimals are compared at their printed precision.
- The Accuracy-AUC column is not reproduced: its frozen source is not among the inputs, and it is listed as such.
- A mismatch stops the run. Any fix is recorded in `DEVIATIONS.md`, with the diff, before the run resumes. No definition in this scope may change.
- The noncommitment replicated groups are recomputed with the frozen `seed_consistency` on the filtered pairs and must equal the filtered frozen groups.

**New output** (H = 4, 10 and 21.5 s), restricted to pairs in which neither system is a commitment rule:
- tied and heterogeneous counts, cross-base, replicated groups;
- same-sign, dominant and endpoint-opposite counts, and medians of |γ|, |delay| and |endpoint|;
- a split by pair type (base–base, base–arm, arm–arm);
- the number of pairs involving a prefix-mean classifier (by root classifier).

The frozen Holm decisions over all tied pairs of the horizon are kept. For a subfamily this can only miss rejections, and no additional error-rate validation is claimed.

## B. Conditional timing measures versus RMSCD (test split)

**B1. Conditional delay on the fixed cohort (Lang-type).**
- CT_fixed = D_H / A_H = Σ v_i c_iJ d_i / Σ v_i c_iJ, where:
  - d_i is the trapezoid delay of Eq. 4;
  - c_iJ is window-end correctness;
  - v are the RMSCD weights.
- It is the mean delay of the clips that end correctly. It is undefined only if A_H = 0 (for example, a commitment rule that never answers within H), and undefined systems are dropped and counted.
- By construction RMSCD = H_eff (1 − A_H) + A_H · CT_fixed. Hence, whenever CT_fixed and RMSCD order a pair differently, the system favoured by CT_fixed has the lower A_H. This is **verified as a check, not reported as a finding**. The reported quantities are how often and by how much the orderings disagree.
- Secondary column: the Lang-literal mean of the discrete onset τ_i (first grid time from which all answers up to H are correct) over endpoint-correct clips, with the same weights.

**B2. Conditional delay to each clip's own end (cache-capped per-clip-end practice).**
- All test clips with L+ ≥ 0 are judged on the grid from δ = 0 to their own end e_i = min(L+_i, 22 s), the answer-cache limit of the record version of the length test.
- The per-clip trapezoid delay d_i^own is computed on [0, e_i]. CT_own is its weighted mean over clips whose answer at their last grid point is correct.
- The share of clips capped at 22 s is reported.
- CT_own is computed once per system and weighting and compared with RMSCD at each H.
- Secondary: CT_own restricted to each fixed cohort E_H (matched cohort). The discrete-onset analogue is also given.

**B1 and B2 comparisons**, for each set, horizon and weighting:
- Kendall τ-b between system values, with signs arranged so that smaller is better for every score and values rounded to 1e-9.
- Over all unordered pairs within the set (and same-seed pairs, secondary): the number compared, the number of opposite orderings and the share opposite.
- The median |ΔRMSCD| among disagreeing pairs.
- For B1, the identity check above.

**Illustrative pair**:
- Rule: the first same-seed pair of set B, in sorted identifier order, whose CT_fixed and RMSCD differences have opposite signs at H = 10 s, class-macro.
- Reported: RMSCD, A_H, CT_fixed, their differences with percentile intervals, and the four endpoint strata.
- If no pair qualifies, none is reported.

**B3. Clip-relative time axis (supplementary; adapted, not a published protocol).**
- On C4 = {4 ≤ L+ ≤ 22 s}, labelled as distinct from E_4, and for r ∈ {0.1, …, 1.0}:
  - AFA: accuracy read at the last grid point ≤ r · L+_i;
  - AAA4: accuracy read at the last grid point ≤ r · 4 s.
- Both are averaged over the ten fractions. They differ only in the time axis.
- Primary comparison: AFA versus AAA4. Secondary: AFA and AAA4 versus RMSCD@4 computed on C4.
- Differences are not attributed to any single factor beyond what each comparison isolates.

## C. Model selection by window-end accuracy versus RMSCD

For sets B, N and A; H = 10, 4 and 21.5 s; class-macro and clip weights. Selection and evaluation use the same weighting.
- sel_A = argmax A_H and sel_R = argmin RMSCD, with ties as above.

**C1. Whole test split (descriptive).**
- Original-sample selections.
- ΔR = RMSCD(sel_A) − RMSCD(sel_R) ≥ 0 and ΔA = A_H(sel_R) − A_H(sel_A) ≤ 0.
- Top-5 overlap as the intersection size divided by 5.
- Bootstrap reselection in every replicate: the frequency with which sel_A ≠ sel_R, and percentile intervals of ΔR and ΔA.

**C2. Split-sample selection on test (primary for selection).**
- Test source clusters are split into halves 500 times (seed 20260928), stratified by the class of each cluster's first clip in manifest order.
- In each split, each rule selects on one half's cohort E_H and is evaluated on the other half's E_H, in both directions.
- Reported over the 1000 evaluations:
  - the frequency with which sel_A ≠ sel_R;
  - the median and 2.5–97.5 percentiles of the signed contrasts ΔR = RMSCD_eval(sel_A) − RMSCD_eval(sel_R) and ΔA = A_eval(sel_R) − A_eval(sel_A), which need not be ≥ 0 or ≤ 0 out of sample;
  - their means.
- A half that lacks a class makes that evaluation undefined for class-macro weights; such evaluations are counted and dropped.

**C3. Development-set selection (exploratory secondary).**
- H = 10 and 4 s.
- Selection on the development cohort; test values of both selected systems, with test-bootstrap percentile intervals and the selected identifiers held fixed.
- Development class and source-cluster counts are reported.
- The development split was already used to tune model epochs and learning rates by window-end accuracy, and post-processing parameters by RMSCD@10. The result therefore reflects a developer's practice, not an unbiased estimate.

## D. Process components by collision type

- Systems: set B, reported as the mean over all 27, the means for round 1 (unweighted loss) and round 2 (class-balanced loss), and the means over the three seeds of each configuration. The frozen trivial systems are included as references.
- Horizons: H = 10 s primary, 4 s secondary; test fixed cohorts.
- For each class k:
  - n_k and the training-set class proportion;
  - A_k, E_k, R_k and RMSCD_k (within-class means, so RMSCD_k = E_k + R_k);
  - S(δ) for δ ∈ {0, 1, 3, 5} s with δ < H;
  - the share of all cohort answers that predict k, at δ = 0, 5 s (if ≤ H) and H;
  - the window-end confusion row.
- Omitted as redundant: the fraction never stably correct (= 1 − A_k) and S(H) (= A_k).
- Cluster-bootstrap percentile intervals for the across-classifier means.
- Per-class values mix recognizability with learned class priors. No claim is made that a collision type is intrinsically recognized earlier.
